Hardware Lists

Internal Hardware Components

  1. GPU (graphics processing unit)
  2. CPU (central processing unit)
  3. Motherboard (circuit board)
  4. Memory/RAM
  5. SSD (storage)
  6. Power Source

External Hardware Components

  1. Monitor (screen)
  2. Keyboard
  3. Mouse
  4. External SSD (external storage)
  5. Headphones

Hardware Cleaning/Maintenance

  1. Keep food and liquids away from keyboard
  2. Unplug PC and deep clean PC case once every 6 months
  3. Clean External hardware, incluing keyboard, mouse, headphones.
  4. Wipe down monitor every week or 2
  5. Clean Surrounding areas
