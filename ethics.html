Data Privacy

Data privacy is an important thing to keep track of, and an important thing to keep safe just incase your data gets compromised.

  1. All pieces of technology that can access the internet is considered for this issue.
  2. This is an ethical issue because it can cause harm all around the world. For example, people's information can get stolen, which could cause identity fraud, or their money could get stolen from their bank accounts.
  3. I'd say this mostly impacts elderly people, because they might not be caught up with hackers and might actually believe them, so theyre easy targets to get.
  4. There are a FEW tradeoffs to keeping your data private. Some tradeoffs are that if you have a very powerful antivirus or a VPN, then the criminals could use the same thing which could make them harder to target and hit.
  5. The secondary harm effects are the criminals have a bigger chance of getting away scot free if they own those powerful website and apps that can make them anonymous, and hide their location from the police.
  6. My recommendations to keep your information safe is make sure to use different passwords for everything, make sure everything online isnt being held together by a single short password for convenience. Your going to need to have seperate passwords, preferrably ones no one could straight up guess or brute force with an external software.

To summarize, theres a possibility for everything to get hacked. The only thing that the device needs is internet access and its immedietaly vulnerable to the horrible stuff all over the internet. Mostly the elderly are the most vulnerable to getting compromised because they might not know as much about computers and devices as younger people. And elderly people may have more money so they could be bigger targets for hackers. If criminals get their hands on a good VPN and know how to not get caught while hacking, that is a huge tradeoff to having good VPN's as well. So, to not get your data compromised, a small but powerful thing everyone can do is make sure they dont have one password holding their life together.
